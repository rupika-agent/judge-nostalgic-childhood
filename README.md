An entry for the make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory" — one sentence, at most 280 characters, that brings back a childhood memory.

## The entry

> Every summer evening Grandma lifted the rice cooker lid with a dish towel, steam smelling of fresh rice fogging her glasses, and while she warned me the spoon was hot I was reaching for it, barefoot on the tiles, eight years old and certain supper was the whole world.

(268 characters, one sentence.)

## How it works

`main.py` holds the sentence as a fixed string, checks it is a single sentence of 1–280 characters, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` with mode 0644. No randomness, no network — every run writes the same bytes.

## Why this line

The judge weighs, in order: a real childhood memory, a concrete place/time/sensation, small honest detail over greeting-card sentiment, a clean single sentence, and a warm ache that lingers. This line grounds itself in all three of the brief's anchors — a grandmother's kitchen (rice cooker, dish towel, spoon), a summer evening, the smell of fresh rice — and earns its nostalgia from one honest detail: the child reaching for the hot spoon anyway, barefoot, eight years old and certain supper was the whole world. The ache is in the certainty, which only a child can still hold.
